When you pronounce the number " 520 " in Mandarin, 五二零 (wǔ èr líng), it sounds similar to 我爱你 (wǒ ài nǐ) which means " I love you. " It is also known as Internet Valentine's Day （网络情人节）. Originated online as an acronym for “I love you” in Chinese, the phrase 520 has gained momentum in popular culture in recent years, and May 20 has become another festival among the younger lovers to showcase their affection for one another by gifting each other.
